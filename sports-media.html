APERTURE · commercial operating method

30 days from assumption to evidence.

Week 1 — Baseline before forecasts

Collect permitted aggregate metrics: monetized pageviews, channel mix, return rate, ad yield, contributor economics and newsletter activity. Record what is missing.

Week 2 — Test meaningful navigation

Compare publisher-approved, team-relevant article recommendations and newsletter placements with existing pathways. Protect user experience.

Week 3 — Acquire qualified fans responsibly

Distribute existing, human-authored editorial through approved social, email and community channels. Attribute costs and repeat engagement.

Week 4 — Make a commercial decision

Measure incremental contribution against the baseline. Scale, revise, or stop. Keep the experiment's assumptions and limitations visible.

GOOD QUESTIONS

Original art and straightforward terms. No false scarcity. No surprise subscriptions.

Is this official NBA artwork?

No. The basketball collection is independent, fictional, original art inspired by the beauty of basketball. It is not affiliated with, licensed or endorsed by the NBA, teams, players or other sports organizations. No logos, player likenesses or official footage are used.

Will I receive physical posters?

No. These listings are digital-only. You can use the print PNGs with your own printer or local print shop. Physical print-on-demand orders will be offered only after a supplier and fulfillment process are tested.

How and when do I get my files?

Use the secure Stripe checkout. Stripe directs you to a secure download page after payment. Your purchase is verified with Stripe before the ZIP can be accessed. If the return page fails or you need help, email support.

Can I resell or use the images commercially?

Not with the personal license. You may use them personally and print for your own space; commercial reproduction, resale, redistribution of files, merch and exclusive use are not included. For business licensing, ask by email.

What size and quality are the files?

Poster PNGs are 3000×4500 px (2:3), phone wallpaper PNGs are 1440×3120 px, and vector SVGs can be scaled to other print sizes. Final print quality varies by printer, paper and selected physical size.

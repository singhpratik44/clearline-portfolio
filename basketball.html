02 / The standard

Four things the game
never negotiates.

I / GUARD THE INVISIBLE

Defense is a promise.

Talk early. Shrink the floor. Rotate on the flight of the pass. Finish the stop with a rebound. A good defensive possession is five people keeping one promise.

II / MAKE THE READ

The open player is the play.

Spacing creates possibility; recognition turns possibility into points. Attack the advantage, move the defense, choose the right shot instead of the loud one.

III / EARN THE MOMENT

Pressure is rehearsed.

Late-clock situations, sideline out-of-bounds, free throws with consequences, switches, traps, and after-timeout execution belong in practice long before the final minute.

IV / LEAVE PROOF

Film doesn't flatter.

Record the teaching point. Name the missed rotation. Recognize the next correct read. Separate effort from execution, and never mistake a spreadsheet for a coach's eye.

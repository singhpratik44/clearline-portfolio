01 / The philosophy

Win the details.
Earn the moment.

A victory is never just the final shot. It is the rotation called early, the rebound secured, the extra pass made, the timeout understood, and the trust built long before tipoff.

01 / DEFENSE

Five players. One promise.

Contain the ball. Help and recover on time. Communicate through screens. Finish possessions with a rebound.

02 / DECISIONS

See the advantage.

Spacing, timing, middle touches, paint pressure, shot selection, and the pass that moves a defense before it breaks.

03 / PRESSURE

Rehearse the last minute.

Side-out actions, clock awareness, late-game counters, free throws, and the ability to remain calm when the game accelerates.

“Pressure doesn't create your habits. It reveals them.”

An adaptable head-coach takeover plan: learn the program, install habits, and let competition test what has been taught.

DAYS 01–30

Listen + assess

Meet athletic leadership; understand varsity terminology; evaluate fundamentals; establish safety, family communication, and team standards.

Deliverable: baseline assessment
DAYS 31–60

Install + repeat

Build defensive rules, offensive spacing, transition roles, special situations, and position-appropriate individual development work.

Deliverable: practice / film rhythm
DAYS 61–90

Compete + adjust

Revisit coverage decisions, scout opponent tendencies, track quality of possessions, and align development with next-level expectations.

Deliverable: coaching review

Purchase, review and placement

  1. Choose a one-time sponsorship package and review its price and description on Stripe checkout. Campaigns are not recurring subscriptions; purchases do not transfer ownership of APERTURE content.
  2. After paying, the advertiser must email APERTURE advertising support from their business contact address with the receipt reference, company name, target HTTPS destination, a short promotional headline (max 60 characters), body (max 160 characters), proposed start dates, and a statement that they own or license all submitted creative and brand materials. Do not email customer lists or sensitive data. No uploads through the public site.
  3. APERTURE manually verifies the payment with Stripe, confirms the advertiser's business, reviews the destination and creative for safety and accuracy, checks inventory and confirms a start/end date in writing before activating the placement. Payment alone does not publish an ad. We will aim to respond to a complete submission within five business days; this is a planning target, not a guaranteed publication date.
  4. The duration begins once the ad actually appears on the live site, not on the payment date. APERTURE should retain an evidence record of approval, live placement URL, start/end timestamps and removal.

What cannot be advertised

Gambling, betting, adult products, alcohol or nicotine, illegal products, hate/extremism, deceptive financial or health claims, unlicensed athlete endorsements, unauthorized team logos or clips, personal information about minors, surveillance, weapons, or external content inconsistent with a youth-friendly sports experience. Ads cannot influence score information, rankings or the underlying product recommendations. Third-party advertiser links must be secure HTTPS destinations, with appropriate privacy disclosures.

Inventory limits and conflicts

The current launch is intentionally limited to a single purchase for each package until scheduling and inventory are proven. Concurrent packages occupy distinct labeled surfaces. If inventory is unavailable, APERTURE must confirm an alternative date or return payment. There is no guaranteed exclusivity within a sport, team, city, sponsor category or user audience, and no demographic targeting.

Refunds and cancellations

If APERTURE rejects creative, cannot provide the purchased slot within a mutually acceptable time window, or cannot deliver the placement, the advertiser can request a full refund to the original payment method. If a live placement is removed early because APERTURE cannot deliver the agreed period, the unused portion will be refunded proportionally, or a mutually agreed extension offered. Where the advertiser requests cancellation after an approved placement begins, refund of the unserved duration is subject to reasonable reconciliation of already-delivered days. No automatic refund is implied by low impressions or low clicks because traffic is not guaranteed. Applicable consumer or commercial laws prevail over these terms.

Data, rights and approval

Ads are contextual placements, not behaviorally targeted. The fan moment builder is browser-local: advertiser does not receive fan name, dedication or private inputs. Submitted logos, product photos, descriptions and links must be owned or properly licensed. APERTURE may reject, suspend or remove harmful or misrepresented creative with appropriate notice. Any advertiser claim that a league, club or athlete endorses an offer requires documented authorization.

THE DETAILS

Good questions. Clear answers.

Is making a fan moment really free?

Yes. You can create, share and print an original text-based fan dedication without paying or creating an account.

Do you sell official league merchandise or highlights?

No. APERTURE is an independent creative marketplace. Gifts are original digital activities; scoreboard cards link to official league websites. We do not sell licensed league footage or team merchandise.

How do I receive a digital gift?

Review each product and the digital gift terms before buying. Existing gift products use Stripe-hosted payment links and public printable content; purchasing does not unlock an exclusive private download.

Can my organization advertise here?

Yes. Three one-time sponsorship packages have live Stripe checkout links. Each requires manual review and confirmed dates before a labeled advertisement is published. Read advertising conditions.

Can I buy a premium personalized animation?

Not yet. Premium payment-verified personalized delivery is being developed. The free fan moment creator is available now, and we will not charge for an unfinished premium product.

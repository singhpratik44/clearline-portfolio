    Professional boundaries

    HUMAN APPROVAL
    Careers ≠ credentials. Sports psychology is not interchangeable with mental skills coaching. Clinical assessment, treatment and protected professional titles require applicable professional licensure. APERTURE does not provide psychological diagnosis or therapy.

    No login, cloud database, payment collection, applicant submission, employer messaging or private medical record storage is present in this public prototype. Data entered here stays in this page's memory and disappears when it closes unless you export it. Do not enter confidential athlete information, especially about minors.

    Every opportunity must pass official-source, identity, consent, suitability and approval checks before any contact. Stripe checkout and professional bookings are deferred until a secure backend, policies and operational approvals exist.

02 / Upcoming renewal watch

Six synthetic memberships have renewals dated within 30 days and are not marked void or disputed. This is a watchlist, not an unpaid-balance amount. Some of the same memberships also have overdue invoices; the two categories are not added together as revenue.

03 / Example owner decision queue

  1. Reconcile first: confirm open items, recent payments, credits, refunds and disputes with the authorized billing system.
  2. Block sensitive cases: do not approve collection requests where a charge is disputed or consent is denied/unknown.
  3. Review renewals: compare contractual terms and cancellation notices before proposing staff action.
  4. Assign decisions: owner records an approve/defer/reject disposition in a local audit register; no customer-facing action is triggered by this demo.

04 / Proposed 30-day measurement plan

Record baseline overdue invoice counts, deduplicated exception types, mean administrative review time, owner-approved actions and dates of reconciliation. Only count real settled amounts if independently confirmed by an authorized processor, adjusted for reversals. Use a conservative attribution approach; do not count the original ledger balance as a recovery.

05 / Pilot qualification gates

Before reviewing any real business: executed scope and data agreement, authorized representative, least-privilege source access, appropriate storage, dispute/refund handling, safe retention and deletion, and confirmation that existing billing software doesn't already solve the problem. The public static demo does not ingest real customer data into a backend. Never provide payment-card data, account credentials, personal contact lists or child-identifying information.

WHAT THE OWNER RECEIVES

A decision-ready diagnostic in 14 days.

01. Invoice exception register

Deduplicated open, failed, past-due and disputed invoice patterns, with source identifiers and review gates.

02. Renewal visibility

Upcoming renewals separated from actual past-due debt, with cancellation and consent uncertainty flagged.

03. Owner-approved action queue

Prioritized exception list, suggested next decisions and draft notices; sending stays with authorized staff.

04. 30-day scorecard

Metrics, owners and a repeatable review routine; only processor-confirmed cleared payments count as collections.

HOW DELIVERY WORKS

Four clear checkpoints

DAYS 1–2

Authority & baseline

Agree on scope and safeguards. No live data until authorized secure handling is in place.

DAYS 3–5

Reconciliation

Inspect only approved, minimized invoice-status fields; confirm system behavior before drawing conclusions.

DAYS 6–10

Decision queue

Flag exceptions and protect disputed, refunded, unknown-consent or already-paid records from action.

DAYS 11–14

Owner readout

Present audited observations and a prioritized follow-up policy. No unauthorized outreach is executed.

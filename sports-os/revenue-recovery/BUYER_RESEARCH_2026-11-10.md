# APERTURE — Phase 02 Verified Prospect Research
**Research date:** 2026-11-10. **Status:** prepared only; no outreach sent, no customer payments obtained, no client problems verified.

## Audit scope and limitations

- **24 verified public operating/location pages, 20 distinct *prospective buying groups*.** Multi-site Tri-Valley theCoderSchool schools are counted as one buyer group; other franchise locations have not had ultimate ownership independently confirmed.
- **Verified overdue billing incidents at these businesses: 0.** Public evidence confirms that programs, membership/registration models and contact channels exist, NOT that any organization has missed payments, weak operations, or willingness to buy.
- **Vendor/billing software:** not verified for any prospect. Discovery question: are existing payment recovery features already sufficient?
- **Business email vs buyer authorization:** business-facing emails published on official websites are not proof of permission for an unapproved sales message. Owner/operator identity and procurement authority must be reconfirmed before outreach.
- **No driving required for initial research:** remote discovery is proposed; no physical visits are implied.

## Deterministic lead-priority heuristic

Signals are not predicted conversion rates. Ranking adds billing/renewal model evidence (8–35), multi-location operating scope (20), verified business email (15), program-type complexity (5–15), and Southern California accessibility (10), with North California locations receiving 0 geographic points. A group is shown only once. Ties break alphabetically. Do not infer that a score means customer pain.

## Decisions and blockers

**Ready:** public lead research, rank, source URLs, review-only drafts, hypothetical 14-day pilot scope, a fictional sample report.

**Blocked:** Outreach sending (human approval of exact recipient/body/channel); discovery interviews (buyer consent); valid price (willingness to pay); first customer/pilot contract; access to real billing data (secure data handling); Stripe billing (accepted terms and explicit transaction authorization); real recovered revenue (processor reconciled evidence).

**Suggested first calls:** Confirm whether existing software already catches failed charges and renewals; verify independent owner authority and procurement; identify the simplest reconciliation metric; ask whether a bounded paid review is worth buying. If software already solves the problem, pivot rather than creating redundant features.

**Outcome:** 0 sent, 0 replies, 0 sales, 0 customer metrics. This register is research, not a CRM with confirmed active engagements.

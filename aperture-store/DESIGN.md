# APERTURE storefront design

The activity kits translate decisions into things people can try together at a kitchen table or on the way to practice. The storefront uses large editorial type, a tactile stack of activity sheets, and a compact path from free sample to the two household licenses.

## Identity and color

Keep clear space around the original APERTURE mark and use its circular geometry as a visual echo in the logic pieces and the court diagram. Use the full mark on paper and dark surfaces. The SVG includes its own dark background and inset border space.

| Token | Value | Application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#0e142a` | Main text and reverse sections |
| Cool paper | `#eff7f9` | Base storefront and information surfaces |
| Cobalt | `#4733db` | Primary action, Family Logic, focus ring |
| Coral | `#f56b4b` | Basketball IQ accent, with ink text |
| Yellow | `#f6cb56` | Selected or highlighted detail, with ink text |
| Muted ink | `#45536b` | Supporting copy on paper or white |
| Rule | `#bfced8` | Quiet separation between content groups |

A study of Shopify in Awesome DESIGN.md informed the tonal division between the story and commerce sections and the use of a consistent pill-shaped action across both. Use ink for the high-emphasis sample band and cool paper for the product and FAQ surfaces. Keep the purchase controls and their surrounding conditions easy to read at either polarity.

## Typography

Use Georgia for editorial headings and Trebuchet MS or Arial for running text and controls. These local fonts avoid a font request before the title can paint. Display headings use approximately 1.07 line height and balanced wrapping. Use 1.55 line height for running copy and cap longer lead paragraphs at 62 characters. Keep the heading and action copy on a solid surface with its own dedicated space beside the deck.

Keep the product names **Family Logic** and **Basketball IQ** visible in headings, previews, and purchase actions. Set the price as part of the purchase label. Control labels use the same sans-serif as the body and a clearly stronger weight.

## Interaction

The interactive deck is the main motion element. Layer the sheets in perspective to express the printable product and move them together when the visitor switches kits. The sheet composition responds over 380 ms, the surface-color change takes 220 ms, and a button press responds in 120 ms. The easing settles quickly without a bounce. Native buttons retain their keyboard activation and visible focus ring. Focus feedback and sample outcomes remain readable in a still state with reduced-motion preferences.

## Components

- Use the same primary pill shape for shopping and purchase links. Secondary links carry an underline or directional cue and a descriptive accessible name.
- Keep a selected deck label and a live caption so the product change is conveyed through text.
- Give each free sample its own prompt, answer controls, and feedback area. Provide reasoning in words for either outcome.
- Show the two products with equal hierarchy and distinct accent colors. Keep the household license and price beside the relevant purchase choice.

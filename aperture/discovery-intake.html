Privacy & operating boundaries

This is a static, browser-executed intake and deterministic analysis tool. It does not use a secret AI API, scrape client systems, send data to a server, or store information in a remote database. The AI prompt is designed for deliberate use with an approved AI service; never paste confidential information into a third-party AI service without authorization. Email transmission occurs only through your own email application. If a real hosted submission inbox, secure storage, automated data retrieval, or server-side LLM analysis is needed, those require an authenticated backend and an approved privacy/data-retention policy.

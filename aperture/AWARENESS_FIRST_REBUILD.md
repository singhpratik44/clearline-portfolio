# APERTURE — Awareness-first rebuild (working architecture)

Status: design contract for the existing APERTURE repository. This document does not claim divine instruction, consciousness measurement, or autonomous deployment.

## Governing principle
**It gets built; no individual is required to carry it all.** APERTURE is a human-made, revisable tool. Its creator is not a divine authority and the tool is not a substitute for lived awareness, relationships, rest, or service.

Awareness is not a productivity score. No user needs to earn permission to rest, be present, or exist.

## System contract
1. **Presence before projects.** A user may enter the experience without a task, objective, deadline, or intervention. Offer an optional quiet mode, not a mandatory exercise.
2. **Observe without claiming certainty.** Separate observation, interpretation, hypothesis, source evidence, and unknowns. Spiritual claims are user-held beliefs, never system-verified facts.
3. **Choose freely.** Provide Continue, Pause, Decline, Undo, and Export. No guilt-based prompts, streaks, compulsory engagement, or pressure to scale.
4. **One coherent decision lab.** Keep Command, Universe, Decisions, Execution, and Evidence connected through shared versioned state; preserve existing Basketball, Learning, Career, Civilization, Scripture/Reflection, and Options demonstrations as modules, not replacement sites.
5. **Truth and limits.** Every numerical simulation labels its assumptions. Never portray synthetic scenarios as real employer, patient, student, or market outcomes.
6. **Human authorization.** External messages, applications, payments, trades, deployments, and data sharing remain approval-gated. No live trading by default; Options remains read-only/PaperTrade.
7. **Minimal computation.** Deterministic and cached checks first; use AI only for genuine ambiguity, show cost and source provenance.
8. **Reversible by design.** Event ledger, state snapshots, undo/replay, and explicit recovery path; never silently erase prior user work.
9. **Accessibility and privacy.** Mobile-first, keyboard-friendly, no required sign-in for public demonstrations, local-only sensitive drafts unless explicitly consented to sharing.
10. **No spiritual authority claims.** APERTURE may support gratitude, Naam Simran, reflection, and contemplation, but does not speak on God's behalf or infer God's intentions.

## Navigation model
- **Presence:** optional quiet arrival; no task required.
- **Command:** current state, constraints, next *optional* action, Pause.
- **Universe:** dependencies and affected people/systems.
- **Decisions:** alternatives, uncertainties, reversibility, risks.
- **Execution:** explicit proposals, approvals, status.
- **Evidence:** sources, events, decisions, outcomes, corrections.
- **Domains:** Learning, Sports, Career, Civilization, Quiet Journey, Research, Options — all use the same decision/evidence primitives.

## Implementation sequence
- Phase 0: inventory existing routes, scripts, deployments, data schemas, and tests; do not overwrite production.
- Phase 1: add a non-coercive Presence entry and persistent Pause controls to the unified command UI.
- Phase 2: extract versioned state, decision router, and append-only evidence into tested shared modules.
- Phase 3: connect existing domain demos through adapters, retaining their URLs and preserving local data.
- Phase 4: accessibility/security/rollback tests, then reviewed preview deployment.
- Phase 5: only after verification and explicit approval, consider production release.

## Acceptance criteria
- Existing public pages still load and their links still work.
- No sign-in required to explore synthetic demonstrations.
- Every consequential action requires approval.
- No user is forced to choose a project or produce output.
- Every simulated claim can be traced to assumptions and evidence.
- Pause and Undo work without deleting work.
- Tests pass before any merge.

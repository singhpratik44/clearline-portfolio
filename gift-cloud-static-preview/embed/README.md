# Embed Gift Finder on another website

A lightweight, no-login gift discovery widget is hosted independently at:

https://aperture-gift-cloud-independent-preview.onrender.com/embed/

It suggests six existing Stripe-linked digital printables and offers a separate free gift experience. No API key, card details, or personal data are collected in the widget.

## Embed code

```html
<iframe
  title="Find a thoughtful gift with Clearline"
  src="https://aperture-gift-cloud-independent-preview.onrender.com/embed/"
  style="width:100%;height:740px;border:0;border-radius:20px"
  loading="lazy"
  referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin"
></iframe>
```

Supports sites accepting custom HTML/iframe embeds, e.g. many WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify and custom sites. Each platform's embed restrictions differ. The embed is **not** an approved listing in their app stores.

## Monetization model

1. Free mini-experience leads to paid standalone digital gift packs.
2. Partners embed the widget in their owned sites and newsletters.
3. Sell setup/customization as a manually fulfilled service only after scope, price and delivery terms are agreed.
4. Do not charge recurring licensing fees until tenant separation, support, billing, analytics and white labeling exist.
5. Do not offer digital product checkout *inside* the ChatGPT plugin. Current ChatGPT plugin rules do not permit digital goods commerce or upsell flows; keep the standalone widget and store separate.

## Verification and safeguards

The six destination URLs were confirmed from live Stripe payment-link data in prior project review. Before announcing instant delivery, verify the actual product materials and receipt/delivery process end-to-end.
Existing production sites and Stripe settings are unchanged.
Track only actual clicks and sales if analytics are implemented with appropriate disclosure.

Questions answered

Simple to start. Clear about the details.

Is the Gift Finder embed free?

Yes. The standard widget can be embedded without a license fee. Optional digital activity packs may be purchased separately through external Stripe checkout.

Can we earn affiliate commissions?

Not currently. Referral attribution, revenue sharing and affiliate payouts have not been implemented. Please don't represent the widget as an affiliate program.

Can we customize the experience?

We can discuss a separately scoped custom installation. No custom features or delivery timelines are guaranteed until agreed.

Does it require visitor accounts?

No sign-in is required to create a free gift. The share link itself may contain personal text, so visitors should avoid sensitive information.

Will it work on every website?

No. Your platform must allow external iframes. Some site security settings or content policies may prevent the embed from loading.

Are paid gifts delivered instantly?

Do not assume instant delivery. Product-specific delivery terms and checkout details should be verified before promotion.

Article VI — Network twin: forecast self-calibration

Calibration is earned, not asserted. Each unit carries a forward classification (rising / holding / deteriorating) issued from leading signals — attribution freshness, occupancy momentum, cohort age — and that call is then scored against the realized occupancy on record. The accuracy figure below is computed live from the canonical state; it goes down when the forecast layer is wrong, not just up when it is right. A forecast layer that cannot show its miss rate is marketing, not governance.

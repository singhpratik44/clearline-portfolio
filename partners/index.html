How APERTURE is organized

Small product teams. Shared operating services.

This is the operating model being built. APERTURE is currently founder-operated; these product areas do not imply six staffed departments.

Product ownership

Each offer has one accountable owner, a defined buyer and a delivery promise.

Shared commerce

Pricing, checkout, license terms and fulfillment belong to one common service.

Shared growth

Creators, partner assets and campaign links support each product area.

Evidence and review

Actual purchases, delivery issues and buyer feedback guide the next investment.

Founder coordination: Parry. Automation supports repeatable work; commitments, spending and customer obligations require accountable ownership.

Can a partner earn a referral commission?

A referral agreement must define eligible products, attribution, refunds, payout timing and disclosure before commissions start. No commission is currently promised by this page. Discuss a partnership.

How are team gifts delivered?

The $99 basketball team license covers one team of up to 20 players. After purchase, email the receipt, team name and player count for the written license and instructions. Fulfillment is manual.

Can I buy several coding lessons?

Request a confirmed instructor and schedule first. A quantity of three $65 lessons totals $195; four totals $260. This is arithmetic at the current unit price, not a new discounted package or a reserved schedule.

How is customer feedback used?

Send optional product feedback. Feedback is private unless you separately give written permission for a specific public quote or image.

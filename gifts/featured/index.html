GOOD TO KNOW

Real answers,
no fine print surprises.

Is the free greeting really free?

Yes. Create, preview and share a personalized greeting link without paying or signing in. It's a digital greeting, not a paid gift redemption.

Are the products physical gifts?

No. Featured products are digital printable activities, workbooks or group licenses. No item is mailed.

How does a paid gift arrive?

It depends on the product. Some checkouts redirect to a publicly accessible printable page; others involve manual fulfillment or a redemption service. Read checkout terms carefully. Verified recipient-specific paid delivery isn't available for all products.

Are my personal greetings private?

We don't require an account or save your greeting in a customer database, but the content is encoded in your URL and is readable by anyone you share that link with. Don't include private or sensitive information.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Are these physical gifts?

No. The featured offers are digital activities, printable resources or licenses. Nothing physical is shipped.

How do I receive my purchase?

Delivery varies by product. Some checkouts redirect to printable pages, while others require manual fulfillment or a redemption step. Read the product description and checkout terms before purchasing.

Can I send a gift to someone else?

You can share the gift discovery page. A verified recipient-specific paid gifting flow is still being developed; do not assume every checkout supports gifting to a separate recipient.

Do I need an account?

No account is needed to explore the collection. Stripe may ask for information needed to complete payment.

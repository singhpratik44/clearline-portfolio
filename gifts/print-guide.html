From a digital gift
to something they can hold.

Your APERTURE purchase opens a printable activity or reflection workbook. Use it at home or save it as a PDF for a local printing service. APERTURE does not currently produce or ship physical merchandise.

Make your physical gift in four steps

  1. Complete checkout for the digital edition you want. Open the workbook page.
  2. Enter the recipient's nickname or first name in the optional dedication field.
  3. Select Print / Save PDF. Choose paper size US Letter, 8.5 × 11 inches and standard portrait orientation. For bundles, repeat the export for every included workbook.
  4. Print at home, or take the exported PDF to your own local print shop. Ask about color covers, double-sided inside pages and simple spiral or staple binding.

Four ways to make it giftable

01 / BIRTHDAY

Birthday adventure booklet

Print the hero storybook with a color cover; add the child's drawing on the first page and staple it like a mini-book.

02 / CLASSROOM

Print the group station pages, organize them into labeled envelopes and include the certificate pages for a celebration day.

03 / ACHIEVEMENT

Framed learning award

Print a Little Victories award on thicker paper and frame it. Add a specific achievement and signature by hand.

04 / PURPOSE & FAITH

Reflection keepsake journal

Print pages for a family member or friend, bind them as a notebook and optionally include blank pages for private writing.

Print tips

  • Standard US Letter paper is supported; A4 may fit but review margins before printing.
  • Use color for the cover and black-and-white for writing pages when keeping costs low.
  • Save a separate PDF for each workbook in a bundle. The browser print view exports the currently selected workbook only.
  • Do not include sensitive or identifying information about a child in files uploaded to third-party printing services without appropriate permission.
  • One-household printing is included in ordinary gift editions; the Classroom Creative Challenge Pack permits use at one classroom/site. Public resale and republication are not permitted.

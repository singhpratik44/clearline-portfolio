Print and fold

  1. Use one sheet of US Letter or A4, portrait. Set scale to 100% (actual size) and turn off browser headers and footers.
  2. Cut around the outside dashed rectangle. Fold at the middle dashed line with the blank sides together. The upside-down print on the back will turn upright.
  3. Glue the blank sides together if you like. Slip the card into an envelope or hand it to your person.

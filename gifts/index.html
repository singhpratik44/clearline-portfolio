WHAT YOU ARE BUYING

Good gifts, clear terms.

Is this a real downloadable product?

Yes. Each digital kit includes printable activity pages, a personalized cover and facilitator or answer notes. It opens in your browser after checkout and can be printed or saved as a PDF. There is no physical shipment, live class, course enrollment or mailed gift card.

How do I give it to a child?

An adult buys the kit, enters the child's first name or a nickname on the cover (optional), and prints or saves the finished file. You do not need to share personal details about a child at checkout.

What if I lose the download link or have a checkout problem?

Contact APERTURE support with the adult purchaser's Stripe receipt and the kit name. We will review access problems and refund requests individually.

Can I share or resell these files?

The purchase is for personal or single-household use. You may print copies for the purchasing household, but please do not resell or republish the booklets. Download pages are shareable links, not a password-protected customer portal.

Is this connected to Roblox, Minecraft, or other game companies?

No. These are original APERTURE creative activities. They do not include brand-sponsored content, game currencies, accounts or licenses.

Digital products are offered in USD as one-time purchases. Stripe handles payment details. APERTURE does not collect child profiles or store the To/From dedication entered on the printable cover. Payment methods, applicable legal tax obligations, and the adult purchaser's local consumer rights may affect the transaction. This static delivery page is not paywall-secured; secure authenticated fulfillment is planned for a later release.

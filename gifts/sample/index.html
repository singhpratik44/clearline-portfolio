Basketball IQ: Read the Floor

Two original, printable decision-making exercises for young players and coaches. Allow 10–15 minutes. No sign-up, payment or child data required.

Challenge 1 — Help or Stay?

Situation: You are defending Player B. Player A drives toward the basket. Your teammate is defending A but is one step behind. Player C waits open in the corner.

Draw arrows for two possible defensive choices. For each, write one advantage and one risk. Which choice would you make if A is a strong finisher? What changes if C is an excellent shooter?

Choice 2: ______________________________________________

Evidence for my choice: __________________________________

Coach discussion: Helping can protect the rim but risks an open corner shot. Staying may protect the corner but leave a driving lane. The best read depends on spacing, help assignments, player strengths and communication. There is no universally correct answer.

Challenge 2 — The Extra Pass

Situation: Your team has three players against two defenders in transition. The ball handler approaches the free-throw line. One teammate runs to the right wing; another cuts to the basket.

Sketch the three offensive positions and two defenders. Show a pass to the cutter, a pass to the wing, and a possible shot by the ball handler.

For each option, write what the nearest defender would have to do. Which option creates the clearest high-quality attempt? What if the wing defender stays home?

My first read: __________________________________________

My second read: _________________________________________

Coach discussion: Look for the defender forced to guard two threats. Compare passing angles, shot quality, turnovers and timing. Have players explain their reads rather than memorizing one play.

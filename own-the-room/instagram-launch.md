## First seven Reels — original scripts

1. **Walk in like you belong** — Hook: “The first five seconds change when you stop performing.” Visual: entering a room, relaxed shoulders. Script: “Exhale. Look up. Find one thing you appreciate in the room. Greet someone. That’s presence.” CTA: “Try the free Walk In reset.”
2. **Stop rehearsing your next line** — Hook: “The most magnetic conversation skill is surprisingly quiet.” Script: “Ask one real question. Listen. Follow up on a detail. You don't need a perfect line.” CTA: “Try Talk Smooth.”
3. **Awkward silence is allowed** — Hook: “Silence doesn't mean you failed.” Script: “A pause can mean someone is thinking. Breathe. Smile if it feels natural. Stay present.” CTA: “Try Stay Cool.”
4. **Respect earns more than volume** — Hook: “Leadership isn't talking the most.” Script: “Name the goal. Offer a useful next step. Ask someone else for input.” CTA: “Try Lead Well.”
5. **20 seconds to reset** — Hook: “Try this before your next conversation.” Script: “Feel your feet. Release your jaw. Exhale slowly. Turn your attention outward.” CTA: “Save this and try the free timer.”
6. **Confidence without arrogance** — Hook: “You don't have to dominate a room to feel at home in it.” Script: “Be warm. Be clear. Respect boundaries. Let people be themselves.” CTA: “Send this to someone who needs a reminder.”
7. **A week of small actions** — Hook: “One social challenge a day beats waiting to feel confident.” Script: “Start with a greeting. Then listen. Then practice silence. Small repetitions add up.” CTA: “Start with the four free practices.”

1. Build your kit

Start with a clean applicator, gentle moisturizer appropriate for your skin, optional sunscreen for daytime, optional complexion product, one cheek color, one lip color and a remover. No specific product is necessary.

2. Your five-minute routine

  1. Wash hands and prepare skin; let moisturizer settle.
  2. Use optional base only where desired; blend from center outward.
  3. Add a small amount of cheek color and build gradually.
  4. Apply lip balm or lip color.
  5. Check in natural light and remove or soften anything that feels heavy.

3. Your ten-minute routine

Follow the five-minute routine, then define brows lightly, add a neutral eye shade if desired, and blend edges. Avoid sharing eye products.

4. Your twenty-minute routine

Practice a two-shade eye look, use clean brushes, and test placement before adding more product. Allow time for removal if irritation occurs.

5. Choose your palette

Rosy

Soft pink cheeks, muted rose lips, neutral eyes.

Peach

Peach cheeks, warm nude lips, soft brown eyes.

Berry

Sheer berry cheeks, berry-tinted lips, taupe eyes.

Bronze

Warm bronzed cheeks, caramel lips, bronze eyes.

6. Spend with intention

Choose a total budget before shopping. Prioritize what you will use often. Compare shade ranges, ingredients, return policies and prices at retailers; this guide does not provide shade matching.

7. Your checklist

□ Skin preparation □ Clean tools □ Optional base □ Cheek color □ Lip color □ Gentle remover □ Patch-test as directed □ Compare total cost

8. Practice and reflect

Day 1: five-minute look. Day 2: try a different cheek placement. Day 3: practice blending. Day 4: compare daylight and indoor lighting. Day 5: organize products. Day 6: create an occasion look. Day 7: choose your favorite repeatable routine.

9. Notes

What felt comfortable? What took too long? Which product could you skip? What will you use again?

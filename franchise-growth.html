Executive implementation sequence

90 days. Three decision gates.

Days 01–30

Baseline

Map the funnel, define qualified lead criteria, identify territory evidence gaps, and agree on a commercial scorecard.

Gate: baseline accepted
Days 31–60

Controlled pilot

Test readiness assessment, territory research templates, and sales-to-operations handoff with designated reviewers.

Gate: pilot authorized
Days 61–90

Evaluate

Compare observed conversion, cycle time, and readiness outcomes; recommend scale, revision, or stop.

Gate: executive decision
Portfolio walkthrough / 20 minutes

How to review this demonstration.

Minutes 0–5: Inspect territory tradeoffs. 5–10: Explore operator readiness and funnel leakage. 10–15: Stress-test unit economics. 15–20: Review decision gates and the proposed 90-day pilot.

APERTURE organizes evidence → dependencies → decisions → accountable execution → feedback. The goal is better commercial judgment, not autonomous franchise approvals.

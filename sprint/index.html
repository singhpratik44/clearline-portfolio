Good to know

Clear terms. No surprises.

Do I need to buy new AI software?

No. The diagnostic begins with the simplest feasible workflow and existing tools. Suggested automation is optional and requires your approval and a separate implementation scope.

What happens after my deposit?

Clearline confirms the agreed work, start date, communication method and information needed. The remaining $250 is due on delivery of agreed outputs. If scope or scheduling cannot be agreed, ask for a deposit refund rather than starting undefined work.

Can you guarantee savings or revenue?

No. Findings are hypotheses until you test them. The deliverable is a practical diagnostic and plan, not a promised financial outcome.

What about privacy and children?

Do not email student identities, medical records, passwords or payment details. Start with fictional or aggregated data. Any sensitive-data work requires a separate approved process and applicable permissions.

Do you offer spiritual or faith-based business consulting?

Our ethical center is truthful work, human dignity, humility, service, and stewardship. People of every faith and none are welcome. We never sell prayer, divine favor, or spiritual outcomes.

Article II — Live demonstration: an artifact through the pipeline

The pipeline holds artifacts that fail consistency, evidence, risk, or conformance review — fail-safe by default. Healing is mechanical (attach the source, arm the reversal trigger); judgment calls still escalate to a named human.

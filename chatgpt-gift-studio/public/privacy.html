Gift Finder & Gift Giver — Privacy

Last updated November 12, 2026.

This application creates physical gift concepts based on recipient, occasion, approximate budget, interests and optional non-sensitive details. It also creates free shareable digital greetings. No account is required. Do not provide private or sensitive personal information.

The application does not intentionally store submitted gift-plan text in an application database or use tracking cookies. The hosting provider may process operational logs, including network metadata, to provide the service. Data sent by ChatGPT to the app is processed on the app server to generate a response. Do not submit payment card or shipping details.

There is no checkout, direct purchase, targeted marketing or order processing enabled. To share a greeting, the sender deliberately shares a URL with the recipient; the greeting's text is encoded in the URL fragment, which is normally not sent to this application's server. Anyone with the full URL can read the text. Do not include sensitive personal information. No contact lists or phone numbers are collected and this app does not send messages. The app does not sell user data. The service may change as a merchant and support contact are onboarded; this notice must be reviewed before directory submission.

Questions: contact the Clearline project owner using the existing project website. A dedicated support email and terms must be added before public directory submission.

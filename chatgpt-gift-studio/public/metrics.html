What these numbers mean

These are aggregate interaction events, not unique people. Share actions do not prove delivery. A completed activity means its reveal button was used, not that an offline act of kindness occurred.

Counts are held only in this server's memory and reset when it restarts or redeploys. They are not historical totals. No names, message contents, cookies, IP addresses or gift IDs are stored in these counters. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control opt-outs are honored.

These figures are publicly accessible. They are not sales, revenue or conversion rates.

# APERTURE Spiritual Center · Operating Charter v1

**Mission:** Create useful experiences that enlarge awareness, joy, love, and wonder while acting with truthfulness, service, humility, care, and responsible stewardship.

This is a *working design commitment*, not a claim of spiritual authority, a statement of belief on behalf of customers, or proof of social impact.

## Three distinct access lanes

1. **Universal human experience:** A person can use education, sports, gifts, confidence tools, and commerce without endorsing any religion. Be welcoming to people of every faith and none.
2. **Optional Sikh reflection:** Truthful living, humility, equality, remembrance of the One, honest work, and seva (selfless service) are referenced in their own Sikh context. For primary study use SGPC's material: https://sgpc.net/sikhism/basic-articles/
3. **Optional Christian reflection:** Love of God and neighbor, justice, mercy, and humility can be explored through Mark 12:28–31 and Micah 6:8: https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Micah+6%3A8%2CMark+12%3A28-31&version=KJV

Do not represent the traditions as interchangeable. Never rewrite scripture as if APERTURE invented it, and never present our own generated reflections as sacred text. Core prayer, scripture references, and simple self-guided reflection remain free.

## Ten linked project obligations

| Initiative | Practice of service | Evidence and constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Education | Patient teaching, equal dignity, opportunity to learn | Guardian consent when needed; verifiable lessons, safe tools, transparent fees |
| APERTURE Network | Mutual introduction and belonging | Explicit permission; no harvesting, pressure, invented partners |
| Funding | Steward resources for specific human outcomes | Separate cash received, forecast, and aspirations; truthful disclosures |
| Sports | Humility, fair play, inclusive practice | Safety, safeguarding, evidence of activities, not invented athlete gains |
| Game Coding | Creative agency and joyful learning | Runnable work, accessible free examples, actual class/product availability |
| Gift Store | Generosity, gratitude, encouragement | Test open-and-share links, distinguish free gifts from paid fulfilled items |
| Human Experience | Awareness, joy, love, wonder without pressure | No fake emotional outcome scores, manipulative streaks, or surveillance |
| Confidence | Courage, presence, respect, listening | No shame, appearance hierarchy, fabricated testimonials, or guaranteed results |
| Gift Plugin | Make meaningful giving easier and safer | No claim of public approval, delivered gifts, or payments without verified evidence |
| Clearline Commerce | Earn from delivered value with fair terms | Verify scope, payout readiness, tax/refunds/privacy, human approval where required |

## Universal decision gate

Before a consequential product change, campaign, partnership, paid offer, or outreach:

- **People:** Who benefits? Could a child, customer, or vulnerable person be harmed or excluded?
- **Truth:** Which statements are evidence, assumptions, or aspirations? Label each.
- **Consent:** Who has authorized contact, data use, publication, representation, and spending?
- **Value:** Is there a concrete, functioning outcome, not merely an attractive checkout?
- **Fairness:** Are costs, refunds, labor terms, accessibility, and privacy understandable?
- **Faith:** Is any faith lens freely chosen, clearly attributed, and separate from commercial pressure?
- **Reversibility:** Can we halt, refund, roll back, or correct mistakes safely?
- **Evidence:** What real usage, successful delivery, learning, or human feedback would change our decision?

**Release rule:** No public success claims without proof; no direct outreach or purchases without applicable authorization; no claims of divine favor or spiritual guarantees for sale. If a paid product is not fulfilled or a link cannot be verified, do not promote it as ready.

## Acceptance test for an initiative

A complete integration means a visitor can find the mission, take a useful action, understand what is free vs. paid, choose or decline a faith pathway, see truthful scope, and recover from failures. Shared navigation alone is a starting point, not proof the individual service has adopted this charter.

Privacy notice

Last updated November 10, 2026. This notice describes the current static storefront and course reader; third-party services have their own policies.

Course access and progress

CODE THE GAME does not provide student accounts. The lesson reader uses a private URL fragment to unlock encrypted course material in your browser. Anyone who receives your full access URL may be able to open your lessons. Do not share it publicly or send it in support emails.

Lesson completion and personal practice notes are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not automatically synchronized to another device or recovered if browser storage is cleared.

Purchases

Payments are processed through Stripe-hosted checkout. Stripe may collect payment and billing information under its own privacy policy. Do not enter payment-card information into our support email. The storefront does not provide a customer account dashboard or an automated receipt-recovery system.

Support requests

If you contact support, the email you send and details you choose to include are available to the support mailbox for responding to your request. Include only the information needed to identify your purchase or troubleshoot an error. Do not include complete access links, card numbers, passwords, or sensitive information about a child.

Children and parents

The products are educational but this site does not provide a supervised student account service. Parents or guardians should handle purchases and any support communications for children. Do not submit children's personal information through support requests.

Third parties and website hosting

GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub and your browser or network provider may process technical connection information under their own policies. Stripe processes payments. Links to game publishers or software providers lead to services with separate policies.

Questions and requests

For questions about information you submitted directly to CODE THE GAME, email CODE THE GAME support. For payment information handled by Stripe, consult Stripe's privacy information or contact support with the purchase date and checkout email. This notice is not a promise that third-party records can be deleted through our site.

# Hackathon Studio Instructor Pack — CODE THE GAME

Independent classroom resource · Version 1 · 4 lessons × 45–60 minutes

The browser tool produces a project brief, not a submission to any external event. Verify official eligibility, consent, and current rules before participating in a competition.

## Lesson 1 — Discover a real need
Prompt: Choose a school or community challenge that can be explored without collecting personal data.
Students identify users, problem evidence, and one ethical constraint.
**Sample:** Students cannot find a quiet study area. Possible solution: a fictional room availability mock-up. Do not publish real student schedules.

## Lesson 2 — Prototype and scope
Use the lab to define the idea, measurable success and available hours.
Students sketch a screen, workflow or physical prototype.
**Evidence:** Three features, one explicitly deferred feature and a simple storyboard.
**Teacher prompt:** What is the smallest testable version?

## Lesson 3 — Test and revise
Conduct three repeatable, consent-based tests with fictional or non-sensitive data.
Record expectation, result and change.
**Evidence:** Three trials, a failure case and one revision.

## Lesson 4 — Pitch and review
Two-minute presentation: Problem (20s) → users (20s) → demo (40s) → tests (25s) → limits (15s).
Students use a 12-point rubric: relevance, feasibility, testing, explanation (0–3 each).
**Teacher feedback:** Ask what evidence could prove the team's claim wrong.

## Suggested classroom judging rubric
- Problem definition (0–3): Is the problem concrete and supported?
- Working prototype (0–3): Can the team demonstrate an idea?
- Testing and iteration (0–3): Are results recorded and used?
- Inclusive communication (0–3): Are users, risks and limitations acknowledged?
This is **not** an external organizer's judging rubric.

## Competition readiness
Official organizer requirements supersede these materials. Verify age limits, team size, intellectual property, hardware, parental consent, accessibility and deadlines before entering. No endorsement, registration or prize is implied.

03 / AI & data literacy

Make decisions you can explain.

Explore a deliberately simple rule-based classifier before studying real machine learning. A visible decision rule helps students ask what information was ignored and where errors occur.

Mission: sort sensor observations

A fictional rescue robot measures light intensity to decide whether an area is bright enough for a solar sensor test. Choose a threshold, then evaluate the decision on six examples.

Rule: if reading is at least threshold, predict “bright.” Otherwise predict “dim.”

Choose a threshold, then evaluate six observations.

Evaluate the evidence

The labels here are hypothetical classroom examples. This is not trained AI, camera surveillance, or a real-world safety system.

Discuss: What if the sensor is broken? Does one measurement represent the full environment? How can biased samples affect an automated decision?

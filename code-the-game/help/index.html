Purchase and course access help

CODE THE GAME is a self-paced digital learning product. There are no live sessions or buyer accounts. After a successful Stripe payment, checkout should redirect to a private access link containing your course key.

Can't open your course?

  1. Finish checkout and wait for the redirect. Do not close the tab before it loads.
  2. Bookmark or save the entire URL, including the part after the # symbol. That part is necessary to unlock the lessons.
  3. Try the latest Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox. The browser must support WebCrypto and gzip DecompressionStream.
  4. If the page shows an unlock error, make sure you opened the original full link rather than a shortened or partially copied URL.
  5. If you lost the link, contact support using your Stripe receipt's email and purchase date. Never email your full card number or your secret access link.

Email purchase support

What should I include in a support request?

Tell us which pathway you bought, approximate purchase date, your checkout email, device/browser, and the exact error message. You may attach a screenshot with payment details and private URL fragments hidden.

For refund requests or billing concerns, email support with your purchase details. Requests are reviewed individually under applicable law; no automatic refund or instant link revocation is available in this version.

Software requirements

Roblox Creator: Roblox Studio on Windows/macOS. Minecraft Coding: Minecraft Education with Code Builder/MakeCode; standard Minecraft Java or Bedrock alone won't work. Indie Game Developer: Godot 4 desktop editor. Software, licenses, accounts, tutoring and videos are not included.

What about privacy?

Progress notes and completion status are saved locally in your browser. This site doesn't provide student accounts or automatic cloud sync. Keep your access link private: it works like a key and may grant access to anyone who receives it.

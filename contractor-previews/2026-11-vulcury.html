90-day accountable business plan

DAYS 01–30

Map & baseline

Obtain permissioned GA4 / CRM baseline, inspect Trustbridge buyer personas and supplier claims, audit content and sales stages, align India-team operating hours.

DAYS 31–60

Pilot & validate

Launch two controlled campaigns: DFM/RFQ search-intent pages and a buyer-specific partner outbound sequence, with weekly QA and handoff checks.

DAYS 61–90

Close & operationalize

Expand the winning channel, document content SOPs, fix weak stage transitions and publish an attributable, cost-aware pipeline report.

Four execution workstreams

01 / TRUSTBRIDGE ICP

Build a buyer-quality scoring system

Accept fit only with part specs, likely volume, decision owner, geography and timing. Supplier verification and quote accuracy stay with engineers.

02 / FULL-FUNNEL CONTROL

Search → qualified visit → RFQ → quote → win

Fix channel attribution, form fields, CRM definitions, stage age and no-owner leads. Separate curiosity traffic from actionable RFQs.

03 / INDIA TEAM CADENCE

Never leave junior staff directionless

Standing overlap block, daily 15-minute priorities, twice-weekly work review, 24-hour feedback SLA, Friday quality/learning review.

04 / VENTURE ALLOCATION

Trustbridge first; Calisade next only after proof

No cross-venture metric blending. Propose transfer of only verified playbooks, with venture-level approvals and clear resourcing.

FIRST 30 DAYS

02
DAYS 31–60

Host up to 12 structured discovery conversations; document values, ownership expectations, work-life constraints, investment range and time horizon. Send individualized research steps and encourage disqualifying poor fits early.

Advance suitable candidates through concept research and franchisee validation. Target 3 permissioned franchisor introductions, with no pressure or promises. Produce a candidate-quality and follow-up register, identifying what did not fit and why.

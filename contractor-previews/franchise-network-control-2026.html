90-day deployment sequence

DAYS 01—30

Prove data integrity

Agree one approved pilot network, export data dictionary, normalize definitions of active location, revenue, conversion, attrition and manager capacity. Baseline 15 representative units. Complete role-based access and privacy review.

Evidence: definitions, exceptions, owners, approvals.
DAYS 31—60

Improve one measurable motion

Test one growth or retention workflow in a controlled 5–10-unit cohort. Weekly plan/do/review loop. Compare cohort to a reasonable baseline or matched control, explicitly marking confounders.

Evidence: denominators, change log, leading and lagging metrics.
DAYS 61—90

Write the scale decision

Document SOP, training, staffing, investment needs and operator approval. Expand only if the pilot clears measurable economic and quality gates. Prepare cohorts for future network-by-network rollout.

Evidence: go/no-go memo, forecast assumptions, accountable owners.

Core network service lines

01 · REVENUE ENGINE

Same-store growth

Demand source, enrollment/lead quality, trial-to-sale conversion, customer tenure, revenue-per-unit, and contribution margin. Investigate variance before prescribing.

02 · FRANCHISEE SUCCESS

Onboarding and enablement

Day-0 readiness, 30/60/90 ramp, owner coaching, renewal risk and manager succession. Standardize the review process, not every local decision.

03 · GOVERNANCE

Execution with receipts

Decision register, SOP version control, permissioned source evidence, exception SLA and human approvals. AI assists analysis; humans retain commercial, legal and personnel authority.

Truth boundaries & proof

Scale-capable design is not a claim of deployed customers.

This scenario demonstrates how APERTURE could model 30 franchise networks and 1,200 operating units. It does not establish that APERTURE has contracts with, serves, or has live integrations across those networks. Direct professional experience includes multi-site leadership within Code Ninjas and theCoderSchool, while the broader network coverage shown here is modeled capacity, not historical operating scope.

Production implementation would require the employer's consent, secured data pipelines, privacy controls, data dictionaries, finance reconciliation, documented KPI provenance, integration tests, unit permissions and supervised execution.

Truth is already true. Evidence determines action.

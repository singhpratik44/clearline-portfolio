90-day accountable business plan

DAYS 01–30

Map & baseline

Confirm AEI 100 inventory, Great Eight permissions, methodology separation, buyer use cases and pricing hypotheses. Research 30 named accounts.

DAYS 31–60

Pilot & validate

Run sponsor/licensee discovery, negotiate a scoped proof-of-value package and draft legal/commercial terms with the founder.

DAYS 61–90

Close & operationalize

Submit approval-ready offers, pursue a first paid agreement, deliver a repeatable qualified pipeline and renewal playbook.

Four execution workstreams

01 / PARTNER INVENTORY

Define where commercial access is allowed

AEI 100 sponsor visibility, Great Eight education licenses, institutional seats and sponsor-funded access. Never sell ranking, selection or inclusion.

02 / BUYER VALIDATION

Three offer variants, one proof point each

Founding Partner: audience + editorial collaboration. Professional License: governed curriculum usage. Sponsored Access: donor-funded athlete founder cohorts.

03 / SALES EXECUTION

Buyer, budget, decision, evidence

Account map, prospect fit, contact, intro path, discovery evidence, approved proposal, dated next action and counterparty decision.

04 / CONTRACTING

Term sheet with integrity constraints

Usage rights, brand review, minimum delivery, renewal and termination; explicit independence of AEI methodology and ranking decisions.

30 / 60 / 90-day embedded leadership plan

Days 1–30 — Diagnose and align

Interview decision makers, validate baselines, map workflow dependencies, identify three material constraints, and agree on a scorecard. Deliver an evidence register, operating thesis, risk log, and prioritized action plan.

Days 31–60 — Execute and instrument

Run two or three bounded improvements; establish weekly management cadence, named owners, quality controls, and clear leading indicators. Test process changes before scaling.

Days 61–90 — Institutionalize and transfer

Compare outcomes against baseline, stop ineffective experiments, standardize effective practices, train internal owners, and publish a sustainable operating playbook with next-quarter priorities.

02 / Delivery commitments

90 days. Three working deliverables.

DAY 01—30

Diagnose reality

Interview owners and managers, reconstruct lead-to-member journey, confirm baseline definitions, identify retention and enrollment bottlenecks.

Deliverable

Validated unit scorecard definition, root-cause memo, owner-approved experiment.

DAY 31—60

Implement one change

Pilot a consistent trial follow-up, coaching intervention and family communication cadence in consenting locations.

Deliverable

Written SOP, named owners, intervention log and first measured comparison.

DAY 61—90

Repeat what survives evidence

Evaluate lift against baseline, test for seasonal/confounding effects, document training and extend only if useful.

Deliverable

Scale/no-scale decision, replicated playbook, downside risks and next-quarter priorities.

03 / Economics and terms

Value creation before a fee proposal.

Value model

Define a starting baseline using the client's approved records. Compute net incremental contribution from additional retained customers, better conversion, time saved and avoided rework; subtract implementation and operating costs. Document alternative explanations and uncertainty. Do not substitute illustrative numbers for a forecast.

Formula: Net contribution = incremental revenue × contribution margin − implementation cost − incremental operating expense.

Engagement structure

Option A: fixed-scope diagnostic sprint, 1–2 weeks. Option B: 90-day fractional operating engagement, typically 10–20 hours weekly by written agreement. Option C: expansion to multiple locations or business units, priced after a successful first-stage review.

Specific fees, hours, confidentiality, approval rights, contract type, ownership of work and termination conditions require a written mutual agreement. No client data or system access without authorization.

04 / APERTURE method

PROVE: evidence becomes execution.

P + R

Pin reality. Reason across dependencies.

Start from the official mandate, a verified baseline, decision rights and unknowns. Build the smallest dependency chain. Expand the plan only as evidence justifies it.

O + V

Operate. Verify.

Change one material process under human ownership. Validate intermediate steps, policies, approvals, protected systems, costs and outcomes—not only the attractive final result.

E

Evolve responsibly.

Learn from real performance. Retire weak processes. Preserve successful methods in a replicable playbook. Keep unsupported claims out of marketing and outreach.

Truth is already true. Hukam is already operating. The work is removing Haumai interference.

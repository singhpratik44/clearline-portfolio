Executive investment thesis

Business problem

Youth-facing activations can be difficult to sustain, compare, and scale when partners, staffing, measurement, and follow-up are fragmented.

Strategic response

Launch one geographically bounded basketball-and-learning pilot with a defined sponsor proposition, school/community delivery partners, and operating scorecard.

Value creation

Participants receive sustained access; community partners gain delivery support; sponsors receive documented fulfillment; teams can assess replicability.

Decision requested

Authorize discovery and pilot design—not a league-wide rollout. Confirm program owner, geographic market, funding envelope, and approval requirements.

Critical dependencies

Signed partner scope; funding commitments; legal and brand rights; venue and staff availability; insurance and safeguarding; youth data permissions; baseline measurement; responsible program owner.

Impact measurement

Track enrollment, attendance, repeat participation, participant experience, partner deliverable fulfillment, and costs. Evaluate longer-term educational or athletic outcomes only with suitable data and methods. Avoid unsupported causal claims.

Decision integrity

Document assumptions separately from evidence. No outreach, contracts, youth data sharing, or use of official marks without authorized human review and approvals.

Official USPA admissions information

Letter for sports academies

Hello Coach,

Every young athlete deserves the opportunity to pursue competitive sports without losing sight of academics, technology, and long-term development.

Through APERTURE, I am developing student-athlete pathways combining project-based coding, robotics, AI, sports analytics, and personalized learning plans.

I am also exploring a referral relationship with US Performance Academy (USPA), which offers an online academic model designed for student-athletes.

My goal is to help families explore academic flexibility around training and competition, meaningful technology and career-building projects, and a clear personal development roadmap beyond athletics.

I would welcome a short conversation about offering a free information session or planning workshop for your athletes and families.

USPA enrollment and academic decisions would remain with USPA. APERTURE's supplemental courses would be offered separately.

Best,
Parry Singh
Founder, APERTURE / Clearline

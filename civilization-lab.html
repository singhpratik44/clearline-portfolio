10 / METHODOLOGY

Truth first. Dependencies intact. Humans accountable.

APERTURE separates facts, hypotheses and simulations; rejects unsupported claims; maps upstream dependencies; requires explicit authorization before consequential actions; and records decisions with their rationale. Continuous improvement may change methods, never rewrite evidence to fit a desired conclusion.

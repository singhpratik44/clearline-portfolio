Implementation Record

Governed before/after cards from real deployments. These results are the reason the CLG systems exist — they are not illustrations of the models; the models were built to systematize what worked in the field.

theCoderSchool Tri-Valley

Education · Multi-site coding academy · 3 locations

Before

Single-location operations with limited visibility across sites. High staff turnover, uneven enrollment quality, no shared operating system for pricing or retention.

After

3 locations under one governed operating model. Shared enrollment engine, standardized curriculum ladders, KPI dashboards, and retention systems that treated the network as one system.

~2× reference-class revenue · 500+ members · 60–70% turnover reduction · 4.8-star rating
Maps to: CLG-01 (Enrollment Growth & Marketing Engine) · CLG-03 (Network Governance Model)

Code Ninjas (Mags Associates)

Franchise · Coding education · Dual-site operations turnaround

Before

Underperforming unit economics, weak conversion, high churn, no systematic product ladder or pricing discipline.

After

Pricing restructure ($129 → $269/mo premium migration), product ladder design, KPI-driven management (enrollment, churn, utilization, ARPU), staff training academies, and lifecycle retention systems (6 mo → 1.5 yr).

Doubled revenue and lead-to-close conversion in under 6 months · 400+ customers on premium path · ~$1.9–2.3M LTV impact modeled from the cohort
Maps to: CLG-02 (Franchise Governance Engine) · CLG-01 (Enrollment)

Kaiser Permanente

Healthcare · Multi-facility · Government-mandated systems

Before

Fragmented staffing panels, MU3 compliance pressure, limited cross-facility governance visibility.

After

MU3 integration with member data / Apple Health pathways, staffing panel solution, multi-facility governance system with safety-first gates and named ownership of decisions.

Multi-facility governance system shipped · Safety-gated, proposal-only, auditable · Served millions of members under MU3 requirements
Maps to: CLG-03 (Clearline Network Governance Model) · safety-first gate design that became non-negotiable across all CLG instruments

These three deployments are the customer proof an investor or hiring manager would ask for. The architecture pages (CLG-01–06) show the systems; this page shows that the systems were derived from real operating results, not the other way around.

Your personal reflections are yours.

The APERTURE Quiet Practice free tools run in your browser. The journal is not sent to our servers and is not automatically saved between sessions. If you choose to export a note, the text file is saved on your device under your control.

Information we handle

APERTURE Quiet Practice does not ask you for a diagnosis, medical history, therapy records, or health information. Free exercises work without an account. We do not use analytics pixels, behavioral advertising, or on-site marketing cookies in this version.

Hosting and checkout

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process standard technical request data, such as IP addresses and logs, under its own policies. When you choose to buy the PDF journal, you leave APERTURE Quiet Practice for Stripe, which handles checkout and payment details. We do not receive your full card number.

Manual fulfillment

Stripe sends the browser to a checkout-verification endpoint that checks whether the purchase is complete and paid. A session ID in the URL acts as your private access link; please do not share it. This server does not store journal writing or require a diagnosis. If automatic access fails, you may email your receipt to parry.s.2324@gmail.com. That support email may include your name, email address, and purchase information. We use it only to respond with the purchased PDF or handle customer support. Please do not send therapy notes or sensitive medical information in the receipt email.

Shared devices

Anything entered in a browser tab may be seen by people with access to your device. Downloading an export places the file on your device. Close the tab and delete local files if you do not want them kept.

Questions and requests

Contact parry.s.2324@gmail.com for questions about purchase-related information. We are not a HIPAA-covered therapy provider and do not offer a secure medical communication portal. For clinical issues, contact a licensed professional.

A small practice for real life.

These are the first three reflection themes from the APERTURE Quiet Practice 21-Day Journal. Take five minutes a day, write in a private notebook or print this page. No email or account required.

Educational self-reflection, not psychotherapy. Skip or adapt activities that do not feel supportive. Avoid putting private health information into shared devices.

Day 01 • Make room

What is taking up the most space in your mind today?

Write a few words or simply notice the thought without needing to solve it.

Try: Find five things you can see, four you can feel, three you can hear, two you can smell, and one you can taste.

Small action: Clear a small physical space, such as a table or desk.

Day 02 • Name the weather

If today's feeling were weather, what would it look like?

Feelings change. Your words can be simple, unfinished, or just for yourself.

Try: Name one emotion and where you notice it in your body. No need to force it to change.

Small action: Drink a glass of water slowly.

Day 03 • A kinder voice

What would you say to a friend experiencing today's difficulty?

Try describing your situation in a neutral, factual way.

Try: Rewrite one harsh self-statement into a more balanced and factual sentence.

Small action: Use that new sentence once today.

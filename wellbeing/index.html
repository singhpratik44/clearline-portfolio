GOOD THINGS TO KNOW

A little more clarity.

Is this professional therapy?

No. APERTURE Quiet Practice provides educational self-reflection and general wellbeing tools. It does not diagnose, treat, monitor, or replace care from a licensed clinician. There are no therapists or appointments offered here.

Is my journal private?

Journal text stays in your current browser tab. We do not upload entries or store them on our servers. Export only to a device you trust. Website hosts may log standard technical visit information; see our privacy notice.

How do I receive the paid journal?

Checkout is processed by Stripe. After payment, email your receipt to parry.s.2324@gmail.com with subject APERTURE JOURNAL. We send the PDF manually in reply; there is no immediate download. If you do not receive the purchased product, request a refund.

What if I need immediate support?

In the United States, call or text 988 or visit 988 Lifeline. For immediate danger call emergency services. These exercises are not intended for crisis response.

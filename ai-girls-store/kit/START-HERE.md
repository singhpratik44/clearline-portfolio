## Make your first concept

1. Open the three portraits and choose a direction that suits your project.
2. Read that character's brief in `CHARACTER-GUIDE.md`.
3. Copy the matching identity block from `characters.json` into a working document.
4. Choose one scene template, replace every bracketed field, and attach the supplied portrait as a reference if your chosen tool supports references.
5. Review the result for identity, hands, clothing, text artifacts, and false brand or endorsement cues.
6. Describe the character as fictional and AI-generated wherever the context could otherwise suggest a real person.

The templates are model-agnostic starting points. They do not include a paid generation service, credits, an API key, video, animation, a rigged model, or a live chat companion. Generation quality and consistency depend on the tool and your edits.

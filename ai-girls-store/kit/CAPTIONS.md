# MUSE 01 — Seven Caption Starters

Adapt these to your own project. Replace bracketed fields. Do not invent customer outcomes, endorsements, or claims that a fictional character actually used a product.

1. **Meet the direction.** Meet [CHARACTER]: a fictional AI-created character for our exploration of [IDEA]. This is the first frame of a new visual world.
2. **Show the palette.** [COLOR 1], [COLOR 2], and a little [COLOR 3]. Today's character study starts with a palette and a purpose. Fictional character; AI-generated imagery.
3. **Show the process.** One reference. One setting. One variable changed at a time. Exploring [CONCEPT] with [CHARACTER], a fictional AI-created character.
4. **Ask a useful question.** Which direction better communicates [IDEA]—[OPTION A] or [OPTION B]? These are fictional character concepts created with AI; we're testing the visual approach.
5. **Explain the composition.** We left the frame quiet so [DESIGN ELEMENT] could speak. A fictional AI character study for [PROJECT].
6. **Introduce the project.** An early look at [PROJECT NAME], using a fictional AI-created character to explore [THEME]. More about the project: [REAL PROJECT LINK].
7. **Invite collaboration.** Building a visual story around [THEME]? This concept uses a fictional AI-generated character. We'd like to hear which setting or format would make the idea more useful: [REAL CONTACT ROUTE].

For realistic AI imagery, use the platform's applicable AI disclosure label in addition to clear copy. These captions are starting material, not automatically posted content.

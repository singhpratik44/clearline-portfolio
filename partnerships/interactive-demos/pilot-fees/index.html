How the comparison works

Revenue is retained sales times revenue per sale. Contribution is revenue times margin. The guarantee pays the larger of the guaranteed fee and the revenue share. The bonus model pays the fixed fee plus the selected share of positive contribution above that fee. A 0% bonus gives a fixed fee.

Break-even counts the fewest whole sales that cover the selected fee. With a zero upfront fee, the result reports whether positive sales cover it. If the revenue share exceeds the contribution margin, each sale loses money under that share. Your team can agree on the attribution window, eligible sales and allowable costs before signing.

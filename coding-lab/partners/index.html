What makes APERTURE different

1. Build → Test → Explain → Improve

Every project ends with a working artifact, test evidence, a student explanation and a revision—not just completed worksheets.

2. Student choice, real constraints

Students choose games, robotics, sports or community challenges, then work within clear time, logic and accessibility constraints.

3. Debugging is the curriculum

Students predict outcomes, run experiments, diagnose failures and compare what changed. Mistakes become evidence.

4. A visible mastery trail

Skills progress from events and variables to conditions, functions, algorithms and original projects. The trail is exportable, not a claim of accreditation.

5. Human-first AI literacy

Students distinguish simulations from AI, verify outputs, protect private data and retain ownership of their thinking.

6. Partner-ready instruction

Each pilot is designed around an instructor guide, demonstration, formative checks, accessibility adaptations and a showcase rubric.

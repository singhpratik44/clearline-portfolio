Build something that matters.

Choose a sport, level, and technology. Every project includes an achievable starter, teaching outline, student challenges, rubric, and showcase prompt. External editors open in their official environments.

Instructor delivery standard

Suggested 60-minute session: 10 minutes demonstrate, 15 minutes guided build, 20 minutes independent modification, 10 minutes test/debug, 5 minutes reflection. Use fictional or consented data only. Students should be able to explain their code rather than merely copy it.

Assessment: Functionality 0–3, coding concepts 0–3, testing/debugging 0–2, explanation/creativity 0–2 (10 points total). Score each category with observed evidence; an incomplete project can still demonstrate learning.

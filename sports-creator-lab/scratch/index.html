Instructor quick start

  1. Open Scratch on a laptop or tablet with keyboard.
  2. Create a court backdrop, player sprite, and ball sprite.
  3. Teach one concept, demonstrate it, then have students modify it.
  4. Use the checklist to assess each student's working project.
  5. Invite students to show their game; do not publish minors' work without guardian permission.

Lesson 1: Move your player

Goal: Build a court and make a player move with arrow keys.

Scratch blocks: when green flag clicked, forever, if key right arrow pressed?, change x by 5.

Challenge: Add left/up/down movement. Evidence: Show your sprite moving in four directions.

Lesson 2: Shoot and score

Goal: Use a score variable, a ball sprite, and a hoop target. On a successful shot, change score by 2.

Challenge: Reset score to zero on green flag. Evidence: Make two shots and show score 4.

Lesson 3: Add decisions

Goal: Add a timer, shooting conditions, and difficulty. Use if then else and touching? to detect the hoop.

Challenge: Add a moving defender or a 3-point zone. Evidence: Explain one condition in your game.

Lesson 4: Test and showcase

Goal: Debug three issues, add instructions, and demonstrate the game to another student.

Challenge: Ask a classmate to play without your help. Evidence: Show a working game and describe one improvement.

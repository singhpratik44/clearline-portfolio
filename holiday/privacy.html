APERTURE × Clearline Holiday Collection — Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026. This policy covers the free Moment of Light holiday experience and the SMS invitation-request form.

Information on the website

Holiday gift names and reflections are processed in your web browser. The gift link can include the names and theme you choose. Anyone who receives that link can see those names, and the link could be recorded in browser histories or server logs. Please avoid sensitive personal information in gift names.

SMS invitation requests

The SMS invitation-request form opens your own email client. The website does not automatically send the request or enroll you. If you choose to send it, the email contains the phone number you entered, the date and the exact request disclosure. It is received in the APERTURE × Clearline email inbox and may be kept as evidence of your request. Sending an email does not authorize recurring text messages: a separate activation and confirmation process is required.

Analytics and tracking

Share links can include campaign tags such as utm_source and ref. Analytics is currently disconnected; we do not claim to measure site-wide visits or paid sales. If a project is subsequently connected, analytics remain off by default. You can choose to permit anonymous engagement measurement, decline it, or withdraw that permission at any time using the holiday page's privacy controls. If your browser signals a global privacy preference or Do Not Track, analytics remain disabled.

When enabled, the analytics integration only sends an allowlisted event name, the public page path, and sanitized campaign-source tags. It never sends gift names, text messages, phone numbers, email addresses, or full URLs containing personalized names; automatic capture, recordings, person profiles, and heatmaps are disabled. A separate vendor may process anonymous events and associated technical metadata. We will identify that vendor and location before enabling it.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell or share SMS opt-in information or personal data with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. A future SMS delivery provider would require a separate consent and privacy review before activation. Request emails are retained only as needed to respond, honor suppression requests, or maintain a legally required consent record.

Your choices

You do not need to provide a phone number to use the free gift. To withdraw an invitation request, request access or deletion, or ask questions, email parry.s.2324@gmail.com. If an SMS service is later activated, its STOP and HELP instructions will also apply.

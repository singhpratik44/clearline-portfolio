Why are we here?

A short reflection on creation, human dignity, and living with purpose through the teachings of Guru Granth Sahib Ji.

1. Creation unfolds through Hukam

ਹੁਕਮੀ ਹੋਵਨਿ ਆਕਾਰ ਹੁਕਮੁ ਨ ਕਹਿਆ ਜਾਈ ॥

Through Divine Will, forms come into existence; that Will cannot be fully described. — Japji Sahib, Ang 1.

2. The Divine pervades all

ਸਭੁ ਗੋਬਿੰਦੁ ਹੈ ਸਭੁ ਗੋਬਿੰਦੁ ਹੈ ਗੋਬਿੰਦ ਬਿਨੁ ਨਹੀ ਕੋਈ ॥

All is pervaded by the Divine; nothing is apart from the Divine. — Ang 611.

3. Human life is an opportunity

ਭਈ ਪਰਾਪਤਿ ਮਾਨੁਖ ਦੇਹੁਰੀਆ ॥
ਗੋਬਿੰਦ ਮਿਲਣ ਕੀ ਇਹ ਤੇਰੀ ਬਰੀਆ ॥

Having received human life, this is the opportunity to realize the Divine. — Ang 12.

Put it into action

Reflect: Where did ego obscure my awareness today? Who could benefit from one concrete act of seva?

Minute 1: Sit quietly and notice your breath. Minute 2: Remember someone whose dignity you can honor. Minute 3: Choose one specific act of service and commit to it today.

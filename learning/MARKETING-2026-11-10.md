# APERTURE Learning — 3× Improved Growth & Distribution System
*Research and action plan, verified November 10, 2026. Public programs remain conditional on confirmed coaches, terms and availability.*

## One coherent purchase story

**Discover → Choose a specific skill → Explore a real project → Confirm instructor and terms → Secure Stripe checkout → Receive live lesson → Review verified work → Choose the next session.**

The public homepage is intentionally seven chapters rather than dozens of disconnected sections: (1) value proposition, (2) subject selection, (3) method, (4) example outcomes, (5) founder pedigree and proof, (6) transparent tuition, and (7) school partnerships and parent questions.

Twenty specializations are separate **subject-specific links on APERTURE** (`/learning/book/?track=<approved-id>`). Every booking page displays the one **existing** Stripe Buy Button for the general `Private Coding & Game Development Lesson` ($65 for 60 minutes), with adult acknowledgment and an inquiry-before-payment guard. **No twenty separate Stripe Payment Links, prices or staffed courses have been created or claimed.** A true subject-specific Stripe SKU requires an approved product/price and payment link in Stripe, plus a verified coach. The preferred short-term enrollment product is an introductory coding lesson, not a prepaid entire specialty.

No tracking pixel or cookies are necessary for this launch; these are non-sensitive channel labels in public URLs. The email inquiry sends only track, age band and a request to confirm terms, not student identity. No subject choice is passed into the current Stripe product automatically.

## Offer architecture

**Introductory one-on-one session:** $65 for 60 minutes, existing verified live-mode Stripe Buy Button. Subject-specific link routes into a fit and terms gate, *then* reveals that one existing payment control.

**Multi-session lesson packs:** $65 × selected lesson quantity; Stripe checkout quantity may be adjusted if the already-created payment link permits it. Do not promise automatic pack booking, discounts or child-specific Stripe metadata.

**Weekly:** Inquiry only until real recurring Stripe subscription checkout, calendar times, refund and cancellation rules, coach capacity and payment webhooks are implemented and tested.

**School/group:** Proposal and quote after confirming service scope, vendor requirements, screening, insurance and instructor capacity. Separate from consumer $65 pricing.

## Truth and legal guardrails

- No invented instructors, testimonials, partner logos or school outcomes.
- APERTURE is **independent of** theCoderSchool and Code Ninjas; former professional roles are experience, not partnership.
- Parent/guardian is the purchaser; do not collect names, birthdays or identifiable student records in a public contact form.
- Paid lessons must not be sold as staffed or immediately scheduled if not operational.
- Confirm written refund/rescheduling policy *before* the user pays, not after.
- Verify third-party marketplace terms before joining; Outschool payments cannot be redirected to Stripe.
- For advanced AI, ML, agent design, computer vision and early-childhood programs, confirm appropriate experience and a qualified teacher first.
- Avoid local business listings that require face-to-face service if only offering online tutoring.

## Two-week execution sprint

**Days 1–2:** Inspect website navigation and all 20 booking links; confirm the Stripe button works in a real device without initiating a charge. Establish a standard scope, refund/rescheduling policy, response window and guardian enrollment terms. Confirm exact lesson booking workflow after payment.

**Days 3–5:** Contact former coaches individually and seek written confirmation of qualifications and availability. Only advertise session times if staffing is confirmed. Prepare a standardized sample 60-minute agenda for Scratch, Roblox and Python.

**Days 6–7:** Claim accurate free ActivityHero and Nextdoor presence. Publish two original project demonstrations and clear “What this costs” post. Create one marketplace class only with an actual available instructor.

**Days 8–10:** Request permission from suitable PTA/community leaders to contribute a free demo; inquire about SAUSD Engage 360° vendor requirements if staffing/insurance can satisfy them. Do not imply endorsed status or spam organizational rosters.

**Days 11–14:** Audit visits to subject pages and genuine adult parent inquiries, paid and fulfilled sessions, delivered parent recaps, repeat bookings and permission-based referrals. Decide which three specializations deserve priority staffing.

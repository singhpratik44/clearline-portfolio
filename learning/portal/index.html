THE DESIGN PRINCIPLES BEHIND THE RECORD

Evidence → judgment → next action.

A polished interface matters only when it helps an adult understand what was really learned. These product rules are informed by current education and HCI research—not measured APERTURE learning outcomes.

01 / EVIDENCE
Show the source, not a magic score.

Every claimed milestone needs an attributable submitted explanation or artifact.

Research sources ↗
02 / HUMAN REVIEW
Make coaching decisions visible.

Coach feedback and next actions should be reasoned, timestamped, and tied to an authorized reviewer.

Why review matters ↗
03 / SAFETY
Keep childhood private.

Use guardian accounts, pseudonyms and server-enforced access controls before any real learning data is collected.

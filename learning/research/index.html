How research changes the product

Four rules for interface integrity.

01 · Human oversight

The teacher remains responsible.

Recommendations are illustrative until a verified instructor reviews student needs, prerequisites and project evidence.

See the private portfolio design ↗
02 · Traceability

No progress without proof.

The proposed evidence chain requires a real submission and a coach review before a milestone is marked verified.

See an explicitly fictional example ↗
03 · Frictionless orientation

Every screen answers what comes next.

The 4D navigation is optional; a searchable text list gives direct access to the same twenty learning tracks.

Explore the learning map ↗
04 · Honest limits

No invented impact.

Research findings are not claims about APERTURE. Actual student records, automated predictions and account access remain off until reviewed and securely deployed.

Read the detailed source review ↗

Project-first instruction

Learn by building.
Master by explaining.

Our independent teaching model draws on widely used project-based learning principles and the operator’s experience leading live coding programs at theCoderSchool and Code Ninjas. Each learner works toward a project they actually care about, with a coach who helps them develop understanding rather than simply follow instructions.

Experience behind the approach

Our founder previously directed theCoderSchool Pleasanton and Code Ninjas locations. APERTURE Learning is an independent initiative—not an official theCoderSchool curriculum, franchise or affiliate. We do not reproduce proprietary lesson materials.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

How do the 20 specializations work?

Choose a track from the Specialization Atlas, then explore a four-chapter learning roadmap and sample projects. The track is a proposed direction, not a preset course, subscription or included certification. A human instructor adapts the path to readiness and interests. Every $65 one-time lesson pays for a 60-minute private session.

Can we buy lessons every week or in advance?

Yes. A one-time $65 purchase buys one 60-minute private lesson, or you can buy multiple at checkout if quantity is enabled. Weekly subscriptions are offered as an inquiry while we finalize a separate recurring payment link, scheduling and cancellation terms. Neither option automatically books a timeslot.

Am I buying a custom pathway or one lesson?

Your $65 purchase covers one 60-minute live online private lesson. The pathway explorer shows possible learning goals, not a fixed-price multi-session curriculum. If Stripe allows quantity changes, purchasing multiple units buys multiple lessons at $65 each. We refine the personalized learning plan after purchase.

Can my child study artificial intelligence?

Age-appropriate AI literacy can be part of a pathway, with human supervision and a foundation in computational thinking, coding and responsible use. We do not promise access to age-restricted platforms or particular educational outcomes.

Does APERTURE Learning meet Common Core standards?

Projects can reinforce specific grade-level Common Core math and communication standards. The site shows sample mappings; we do not claim accreditation or that every coding lesson satisfies all Common Core requirements.

Where are lessons held?

The $65 private lesson is a live online session, arranged with the family after purchase. In-person offerings may be announced separately after suitable venues, scheduling and permissions are confirmed.

How does enrollment work?

For private lessons, purchase one or more $65 sessions through Stripe. We then contact you by email to coordinate availability and individual learning goals; payment itself does not book a date or time. For group programs or school partnerships, request a tailored quote first.

Is this theCoderSchool methodology?

Our founder brings practical experience directing theCoderSchool programs. We use original, project-based instruction shaped by that experience and by general teaching principles. We do not claim ownership of or affiliation with theCoderSchool’s proprietary curriculum.

How are payments handled?

Private coding lessons are purchased through Stripe at $65 per 60-minute session, with no subscription required. The Stripe buy button opens Stripe-controlled checkout; this website does not collect or store card numbers. Lesson time(s) are arranged by email afterward.

Is this affiliated with Code Ninjas or theCoderSchool?

No. APERTURE Learning is an independent proposed education service and is not owned, operated or endorsed by those organizations.

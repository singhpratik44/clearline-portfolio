Project-first instruction

Learn by building.
Master by explaining.

Our independent teaching model draws on widely used project-based learning principles and the operator’s experience leading live coding programs at theCoderSchool and Code Ninjas. Each learner works toward a project they actually care about, with a coach who helps them develop understanding rather than simply follow instructions.

Experience behind the approach

Our founder previously directed theCoderSchool Pleasanton and Code Ninjas locations. APERTURE Learning is an independent initiative—not an official theCoderSchool curriculum, franchise or affiliate. We do not reproduce proprietary lesson materials.

01

Listen and discover

Start with a student’s interests, confidence, skill level and one motivating project goal.

02

Plan the build

Break the project into achievable milestones with a visible roadmap and foundational concepts.

03

Coach live

A human instructor asks questions, models debugging, introduces concepts and gradually reduces hints.

04

Make, test, iterate

Students build working artifacts, troubleshoot failures, playtest and improve based on feedback.

05

Demonstrate mastery

Explain the logic, show the finished project and identify what the student can do independently.

06

Grow the portfolio

Document milestones and select the next meaningful challenge. Families receive clear progress summaries.

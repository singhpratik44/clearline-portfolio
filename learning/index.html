PARENT QUESTIONS

Know exactly what comes next.

Does the $65 payment purchase a specialization?

No. It buys one 60-minute private coding and game-development lesson using the existing Stripe product. The 20 subject areas are possible personalized directions, not separate staffed courses or all-inclusive purchases.

Can we choose Roblox, Python, AI or another subject?

Yes, you can select a preferred pathway and send an inquiry. Instructor qualifications, technical requirements and subject availability must be confirmed. Advanced programs may require foundational skills and a specific instructor.

What ages are appropriate?

Many starter pathways are aimed at school-age learners around 7 and up, while advanced AI topics are aimed at teens. For ages 4–6, ask about suitability before purchasing. The parent or guardian handles enrollment.

How do scheduling, cancellations and refunds work?

Payment does not book an appointment. Please request instructor availability and written scheduling, cancellation, missed-lesson and refund conditions before payment. Until these are confirmed, do not assume a time slot or automatic refund eligibility.

Do you have verified testimonials or school partnerships?

Founder experience is described separately from APERTURE partnerships. We publish a testimonial, learner example or partner identity only after verification and appropriate permission.

How is student information protected?

Our public project demonstrations use fictional examples and do not require a student's full name. A private portfolio feature is being built separately and remains disabled until Supabase security, guardian and coach authorization, and data safeguards are verified.

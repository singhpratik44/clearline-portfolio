03 / AI literacy · Data & model behavior

Teach a tiny classifier. Then question its predictions.

A simple 3-nearest-neighbors demonstration classifies a fictional snack from two measurements. Try changing the values to see how examples influence the prediction—and why real AI needs good data, testing and human judgment.

Change the sliders and press Predict category.
What examples does this model use?

Three training examples labeled “crunchy” and three labeled “soft.” It measures the distances to the three closest examples, then takes their majority label. This is a small teaching demonstration, not a production-grade AI system.

Training examples (sweetness, crunch)
Crunchy: (7,9), (8,8), (5,9)
Soft:    (9,2), (8,1), (6,3)
Prediction: majority label of 3 nearest points

No personal data is entered or sent to a server. Real-world AI projects also require data privacy, evaluation, fairness and appropriate adult supervision.

# APERTURE LEARNING — 4D UNIVERSE MASTER PROMPT (THREE-PASS IMPROVEMENT)

**Mission:** Transform APERTURE Learning from a traditional coding-course website into a cinematic, educationally meaningful, accessible, interactive *four-dimensional learning journey*: three spatial axes for an explorable network of 20 specializations, plus learning progression as the fourth (time) dimension.

## Pass 1 — 3× better narrative and parent experience

Build around one continuous story:

**Interest → Specialization → First project → Skills demonstrated → Meaningful capstone → Next possibility.**

A child is the central creator; the brand is a mentor, not the protagonist. Parents should feel the curiosity and quickly understand the offer. Replace disconnected marketing cards with five constellations of real learning directions:

1. **Creative Worlds:** Scratch, Roblox, Minecraft, 3D game design.
2. **Code Builders:** Python, websites, app interfaces, data science.
3. **AI Frontiers:** AI literacy, machine learning, agents, computer vision.
4. **Engineering & Systems:** Robotics, electronics, digital safety, math simulations.
5. **Real-World Creators:** Sports analytics, climate/community tech, creative media, young product design.

Use the founder's real coding-center leadership experience as pedigree without suggesting current affiliation, partner endorsement or proprietary curriculum rights. Display actual testimonials and partner logos only after consent and confirmation.

## Pass 2 — 3× better interaction and visual meaning

Build a **working** 4D universe, not a prerendered illustration or a passive animation.

- Dimensions 1–3: 3D spatial positions (X, Y, Z) for specializations and their family clusters; support pointer/touch orbit, zoom, reset, perspective/depth, selected-node highlighting, and connections between related subjects.
- Dimension 4: time/progression, operated by a labeled four-chapter slider: **Discover → Build → Understand → Create**. Moving through time must visibly transform the constellation's positions/trajectories and change each specialization's milestone story.
- Show four stage markers or a trajectory for the selected path, indicating possible progression, *not measured student performance or a guaranteed chronological schedule*.
- Offer an intentional cinematic moment, but **never force motion**: playback off by default; pause/play and chapter stepping. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, tab visibility and mobile device performance.
- Give a non-visual equivalent: all 20 tracks accessible as labelled, focusable controls with an accessible text detail panel.
- Every path has an explorable story, illustrative student project, potential capstone, and functional click-through into the specialization catalog or live coding demos.
- Optimize for iPhone screens. Keep graphical rendering lightweight and do not require paid APIs or WebGL capabilities.

### Emotional and aesthetic direction

Editorial, modern and luminous: deep marine navy, jade, warm mint, restrained gold, spacious typography, gently glowing pathways, clear contrast. Feeling: *a museum of possible futures crossed with a living engineering lab*. No toy dashboard clichés, excessive claims, busy effects, fake student portraits, false success stories, or unverified partnerships.

## Pass 3 — 3× better business architecture, credibility and operational quality

A beautiful prototype is insufficient unless an authorized guardian can progress to a real service.

**Commercial truth:**
- Existing Stripe live one-time checkout: **$65 for one live 60-minute private online coding lesson**. Quantity, where Stripe supports it, purchases more 60-minute lessons; do not imply a prepaid full specialization for $65.
- Weekly recurring lessons may be offered as an *inquiry* until a separate recurring Stripe price/link and the instructor schedule and missed-session/cancellation/refund policies are complete.
- School clubs, district programs and sponsorships receive distinct proposals, procurement and invoices.
- Nothing asks a minor to purchase independently or discloses a child’s identifying information in a public URL.

**Operational readiness:**
- Real coach roster with individually confirmed expertise, availability, compensation and safeguarding expectations.
- Booking that does not silently collect money without a deliverable time window.
- Guardian consent and private progress-record architecture before storing any actual child's data.
- Measurable deliverables per lesson: starting skill, created artifact, explanation/debugging, parent summary and next step.
- Authentic testimonials and partnerships only after written authorization.
- Explicit refund, rescheduling and billing terms before broader paid acquisition.

**Analytics:** Aggregate, privacy-respecting funnel: atlas visit → world viewed → track explored → demo opened → guardian inquiry → verified purchase → lesson fulfilled → next lesson. Do not track or profile children for advertising.

**Go-to-market:** Warm teacher/coach referrals; parent-organization-approved STEM showcases; permitted Orange County parent/community groups; local small-business channels; precise SEO pages such as "Roblox coding lessons," "Python for middle school," and "responsible AI for teens." Use original working demos as the advertisement. Grow carefully from two or three genuinely staffed tracks rather than marketing all twenty as instantly available.

1. A parent immediately sees how the constellation represents twenty possible learning directions.
2. They can rotate the constellation and tap a specialization.
3. The fourth-axis slider changes both the visual state and selected specialization's learning chapter.
4. Choosing a track reveals a real, relevant curriculum story and potential capstone.
5. The complete pathway and an actual interactive demo open from that selection.
6. The $65 single-lesson checkout is never confused with a full course or automatic weekly subscription.
7. Lower-powered mobiles and reduced-motion users still get a useful, stable experience.

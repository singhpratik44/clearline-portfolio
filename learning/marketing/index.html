LAUNCH RULES

Five messages. One trustworthy offer.

The first goal is a real inquiry from a parent or potential instructor—not clicks from children or paid traffic that you cannot fulfill.

  1. Confirm actual instructor capacity and availability before listing scheduled classes.
  2. Use authorized, permission-based PTA and school outreach—not bulk messaging.
  3. For Outschool classes, use Outschool's payment system rather than outside Stripe checkout.
  4. Do not invent student testimonials, affiliated schools or completed projects.

03 · THIRTY-DAY PLAN

What to do first.

  1. Week 1: verify two instructors, their real availability, contract terms, safeguarding and lesson fulfillment; ensure the correct Stripe merchant is connected.
  2. Week 2: publish four short videos demonstrating original Scratch, Python, Roblox and AI learning examples; link each to the appropriate subject page.
  3. Week 3: seek permission for parent-organization STEM showcases, complete ActivityHero/Nextdoor eligibility steps, and contact a small number of school program leads.
  4. Week 4: deliver permitted live demonstrations, measure qualified parent inquiries, confirmed lesson bookings, completed coaching sessions and honest feedback.

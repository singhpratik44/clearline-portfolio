Honest work and sharing

In a salok on Ang 1245, Guru Nanak turns attention from outward signs of spiritual authority to the person who works for a living and shares what they have.

Look for the line beginning ਘਾਲਿ ਖਾਇ. What does the person do with the fruit of their work?

Becoming a neighbor

BEFORE YOU BEGIN

One ordinary practice.

This original workbook is a personal planning aid, not a religious authority. It does not quote or interpret Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji, prescribe an official Sikh practice, or promise spiritual outcomes. To study Gurbani, consult a trusted independent source or a qualified teacher. The journal, prayer and free 4D journey remain available without buying this workbook.

How to use it

Move through one worksheet each month, or at your own pace. Write privately on paper or save a copy as a PDF. No login is required and these pages do not collect or store entries.

What counts as progress?

Nothing here assigns a score. The pages make it easier to notice truth, choose meaningful actions, and return after interruptions without guilt.

Personal privacy

Anything handwritten or added to your printed PDF is yours to protect. This is a printable guide; it is not the encrypted online journal. Do not enter private reflections into an untrusted shared device.

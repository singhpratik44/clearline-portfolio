A QUIET JOURNEY · SAMPLE

The art of arriving.

Month one · A small practice in truth and attention

✺
Begin where you are, without needing to perform.

01 · NOTICE

What is asking for your honest attention in this season? What do you know, and what are you only assuming?

02 · CARE

Who might be affected by your next decision? What would a compassionate, responsible response look like?

03 · ACT

Choose one achievable action you can take within the next 24 hours. It may be listening, repairing, thanking or doing an overdue task.

04 · REFLECT

What would you like to notice once you have acted? You do not need a score or perfect streak.

05 · RELEASE

What outcome can you stop trying to control while remaining responsible for your part?

Original reflection prompts · not scripture, spiritual authority, therapy or a prediction. Use this page freely.

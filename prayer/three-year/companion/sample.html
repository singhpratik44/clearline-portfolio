YEAR ONE · MONTH ONEAN ORDINARY BEGINNING

The art
of arriving.

Begin where you are.

It is enough to notice what is real today. The point of this exercise is not to become someone new in an afternoon, but to create room for honest observation, responsibility and care.

1. Notice

What is asking for your honest attention in this season?

2. Separate fact from story

Write down one thing you know to be true. Then write a story or assumption you may be adding.

3. Choose one small act

What simple action could express care or integrity today without expecting recognition?

4. Release

What result can you stop forcing while still taking your next responsible step?

These original reflection prompts are not scripture, religious teaching, counseling, or an assessment of spiritual worth.

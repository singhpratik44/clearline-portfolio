The Art
of Arriving

A gentle practice for noticing what is here, appreciating what is possible, and choosing one meaningful next step.

Begin here.

You do not need to feel peaceful, grateful, or certain to begin. This is an invitation to meet the present moment honestly. Grief, difficulty, and rest all belong in a human life.

How to use this worksheet

Write freely or reflect silently. Take the four questions in any order. Answer them now or revisit them throughout the month. There are no scores, streaks, or correct answers.

Part three · everyday practice

Five minutes to arrive.

Choose a pace that fits your body and circumstances. Any step can be skipped.

01
NOTICE
Look around or feel your feet on the ground. Name what is here.
02
APPRECIATE
Find one genuine source of support, beauty, or possibility—or acknowledge difficulty.
03
CHOOSE
Ask what matters most for the next few minutes. One action is enough.
04
ACT OR REST
Take that action gently, ask for support, or choose to recover.
05
RELEASE
Allow unfinished ideas to wait. Return to the present.

Revisit throughout the month

Week 1: Notice. Week 2: Appreciate. Week 3: Choose. Week 4: Integrate. Return in any order, as often or as rarely as is useful.
